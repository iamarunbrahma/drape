## Description

```
Drape answers one question from a single selfie: which colors actually suit you?

Try it: https://drape-youcam.vercel.app

It reads the real color of your skin, eyes, lips and hair with Perfect Corp's YouCam API, places you in one of twelve color seasons, and paints those colors onto your own photo with virtual try-on. Then it ranks 353 real garments you can buy by how close their color actually is to yours, measured from each retailer's own fabric swatch rather than guessed from a product title.

Two things here that other color tools do not do.

The first is that it tells you how much to trust it. When a reading lands near the boundary between two seasons it says so and shows the number, instead of printing a verdict. In this demo the read scores 41 out of 100 because the measurement sits 1.4 from a boundary. Overrule any of the three axes and the season recalculates in your browser, instantly and at no cost, and the score climbs to 84.

The second is that it works across skin tones, and we show our working. We tested the engine against the Monk Skin Tone Scale and it failed: the original rule read darkness as coolness, and it got five of the ten reference tones wrong. We rebuilt it around the hue angle and published the before and after, computed live from the shipping code.

Also in the studio: your own hair color held against the shades your season wears and recolored with YouCam's hair endpoint, eleven dermatologist-grade skin scores, and a complete outfit generated for your season.
```

## How the narration was made

| | |
|---|---|
| Service | Sarvam AI text-to-speech, `POST https://api.sarvam.ai/text-to-speech` |
| Model | `bulbul:v3` |
| Voice | `ashutosh` (male) |
| Language | `en-IN` |
| Pace | `1.12` base, varied per beat between about 1.04 and 1.18 |
| Pitch | not sent. v3 rejects `pitch` and `loudness` outright |
| Calls | one per narration beat (20), not one long call |
| Join | 0.45 s of silence between beats |
| Master | `loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5:LRA=11` at mux, landing at -16.1 LUFS |

Rebuild with `node --env-file=.env.local scripts/build-voiceover.mjs`.

We first used `bulbul:v2` with the voice `karun`, which supports `pitch` and gave the read
a wider emotional range. It was dropped because v2 swallows the D in "Drape" and reads it
as a word we could not ship. v3 pronounces it correctly but offers no pitch control, so the
delivery arc now comes from pace variation alone.
